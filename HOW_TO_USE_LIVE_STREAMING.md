# How to Use Live Streaming - Quick Guide

## 🎓 For Instructors

### Step 1: Schedule a Live Class
1. Go to **Live Classes** page
2. Click **"Schedule Class"** button
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Title**: Name of your class
   - **Course**: Select from your courses
   - **Scheduled Time**: When you want to start
   - **Description**: What students will learn (optional)
   - **Options**: Enable waiting room, recording, etc.
4. Click **"Schedule Now"**

### Step 2: Start Your Live Class
1. Go to **Live Classes** page
2. Find your scheduled session
3. Click the **"START SESSION"** button (blue)
4. You'll be redirected to the setup page

### Step 3: Setup Your Camera & Mic
1. Click **"Enable Camera & Mic"**
2. Allow browser permissions
3. Check your video preview
4. Click **"Start Class"**

### Step 4: Teach Live!
- Your video is now streaming to students
- Use the controls at the bottom:
  - 🎤 Mute/Unmute microphone
  - 📹 Turn camera on/off
  - 🖥️ Share your screen
  - 🔴 Start/Stop recording
  - 📞 End session

### Step 5: End the Session
1. Click the red phone icon
2. Confirm you want to leave
3. Session status changes to "ended"
4. Recording is saved (if enabled)

---

## 👨‍🎓 For Students

### Step 1: Find Live Classes
1. Go to **Live Classes** page
2. Look for sessions with **"LIVE NOW"** badge (red)
3. Or see upcoming scheduled sessions

### Step 2: Join a Live Class
1. Click **"JOIN LIVE CLASS"** button
2. You'll see the setup page
3. Click **"Enable Camera & Mic"** (optional)
4. Click **"Join Class"**

### Step 3: Learn Live!
- See instructor's video
- See other students (if enabled)
- Use chat to ask questions
- Raise your hand to speak
- Take notes

### Step 4: Leave When Done
- Click the red phone icon to leave
- Or just close the browser tab

---

## 🎯 Session Status Guide

### 🔵 SCHEDULED
- Session is planned but not started yet
- Shows scheduled date/time
- **Instructor**: Can click "START SESSION"
- **Student**: Must wait for instructor to start

### 🔴 LIVE NOW
- Session is currently active
- **Everyone**: Can click "JOIN LIVE CLASS"
- Real-time video/audio streaming
- Chat and interactions enabled

### ⚫ ENDED
- Session has finished
- **If recorded**: Can watch recording
- **If not recorded**: No longer available

## 💡 Tips & Best Practices

### For Instructors:
- ✅ Test your camera/mic before starting
- ✅ Schedule sessions in advance
- ✅ Enable recording for students who miss it
- ✅ Use waiting room for better control
- ✅ Share your screen when teaching concepts
- ✅ Monitor chat for questions
- ✅ End session properly (don't just close browser)

### For Students:
- ✅ Join a few minutes early
- ✅ Use headphones to avoid echo
- ✅ Mute yourself when not speaking
- ✅ Use chat for questions
- ✅ Raise hand before speaking
- ✅ Close other apps for better performance

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Can't See Video?
1. Check browser permissions (camera/mic)
2. Refresh the page
3. Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
4. Check your internet connection

### Can't Hear Audio?
1. Check your speakers/headphones
2. Unmute in the browser tab
3. Check system volume
4. Ask instructor to unmute

### Session Won't Start?
1. Make sure you're the instructor/host
2. Check if session is already live
3. Refresh the page
4. Contact support if issue persists

### Poor Video Quality?
1. Close other apps/tabs
2. Move closer to WiFi router
3. Disable your video if not needed
4. Ask others to disable video
